How it works

1

Rank

The recommendation engine scores each candidate against each internship using semantic and structured matching.

2

Allocate

The admin uses ranked output and seat availability to bulk allocate candidates into the interview pipeline.

3

Verify

Companies schedule interviews, verify profiles, send offers, and students accept only one final offer.

Impact

Why It Feels Real

This flow mirrors actual placement logic: multiple interviews can happen in parallel, offers can come from multiple companies, and one acceptance triggers reallocation for the others.

Why It Is Practical

The architecture stays lightweight for hackathons and demos while still showing AI-assisted ranking, transparent scoring, admin control, and company verification.
